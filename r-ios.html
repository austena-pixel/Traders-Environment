Deliberate Routines

Design the behaviour you intend to practise. R-IOS records what happens, compares it with your intention, and uses the evidence to support reflection and development.

Intention → execution → observation
Intention and observation stay distinct.Your plan describes what you intend to do. Execution records capture what you actually did. A delay or skipped activity is not labelled psychological resistance unless you record that experience.
